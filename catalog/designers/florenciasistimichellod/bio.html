Florencia Sisti Michellod is a visual communication and type designer based in Buenos Aires, Argentina. She is the co-founder of FONTANASISTI, an independent design studio working across branding, editorial design, and visual communication. She holds a Master's degree in Typography from the University of Buenos Aires (UBA). Her practice brings together typographic research and design, exploring how letterforms can respond to specific communication needs. Her typeface Sistype was selected for the 7ma Bienal Tipos Latinos. In 2026, she led the organization of the Buenos Aires (CABA) venue of the 10ma Bienal Tipos Latinos, coordinating its program, events, and activities.
