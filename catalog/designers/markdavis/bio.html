Mark Davis is a Brooklyn-based type designer and the founder of WORDMARK, a studio for custom typefaces and logotypes. He studied type design at The Cooper Union and interned at The Font Bureau before shaping brand identities at BuzzFeed News and NBCUniversal. He is the font engineer of the new Geist fonts for Vercel, and his variable-font work includes Cal Sans for Cal.com. Other clients and collaborations include Lausanne for WELTKERN®, Claude Type, Pentagram, Nike, the National Museum of Women in the Arts, and the Kentucky Derby.
