RixFont is a Korean type design company specializing in Hangul. They have collaborated with a wide range of leading brands — including NAVER, Samsung Electronics, Hyundai Card, Kakao, CJ, Seoul Milk, MBC, Daishin Securities, Lotte Shopping, and Hyundai Group — to develop custom typefaces. They also provide a high-quality font subscription service for professionals.
