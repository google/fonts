Darley Sans is a modern, clean, and versatile sans-serif typeface designed for both digital and print applications. It features a balanced geometry and excellent legibility across various screen sizes.

The typeface supports English and Vietnamese, providing full coverage for essential characters, diacritics, and punctuation marks required for these languages.

Designed by Duong Nguyen.

To contribute, see github.com/googlefonts/darleysans.
