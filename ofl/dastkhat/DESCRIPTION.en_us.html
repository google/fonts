Dastkhat (دستخط, "handwriting") is a Persian and Latin sans serif designed for product interfaces, e-commerce pages and dense Persian text on screens. It has a taller letter body, slightly wider Persian letters and digits, a Latin set scaled to sit well beside Persian, and geometric dots instead of the usual stretched ones.

It covers Persian, Arabic, Urdu basics and Latin, in nine weights from Thin to Black on a single variable axis. Dastkhat is a derivative of Vazirmatn by Saber Rastikerdar, and its Latin glyphs descend from Roboto.

To contribute to the project, visit github.com/dastyar-io/dastkhat-font.
