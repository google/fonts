Dastkhat Bar (دستخط, "handwriting") is a Persian and Latin sans serif designed for product interfaces, e-commerce pages and dense Persian text on screens. It has a taller letter body, slightly wider Persian letters and digits, a Latin set scaled to sit well beside Persian, and geometric dots instead of the usual stretched ones. In Dastkhat Bar, neighbouring dots merge: two dots become a bar, three become a bar and a square. It suits logos, headlines and brand marks.

It covers Persian, Arabic, Urdu basics and Latin, in nine weights from Thin to Black on a single variable axis. Dastkhat is a derivative of Vazirmatn by Saber Rastikerdar, and its Latin glyphs descend from Roboto.

To contribute to the project, visit github.com/dastyar-io/dastkhat-font.
