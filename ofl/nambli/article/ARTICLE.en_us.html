Nambli is a rounded display typeface for branding, headings and short passages of text. Its soft terminals, circular bowls and open forms give the family a friendly, clear voice.

The family includes six weights from Light to ExtraBold, each paired with an italic. Extended Latin and Cyrillic characters, including Ukrainian, share the same rounded design vocabulary. Localized forms and combining-mark positioning support multilingual setting.

Nambli was developed under the creative direction of Yurii Tor, with AI-assisted outline programming and font engineering. Successive visual reviews shaped its letter construction, joins, tails, proportions and stroke weights.

To contribute to the project, visit github.com/Tor-Production/nambli-font.
