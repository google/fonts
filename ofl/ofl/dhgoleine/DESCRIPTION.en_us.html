Dh Goleine is a contemporary sans-serif typeface designed by Dharmas Foundry. Built for versatility and elegance, it comes in a complete family of 8 weights — from Thin to ExtraBold — each with a matching Italic, offering designers full flexibility for both display and body text use.

With clean geometry and refined proportions, Dh Goleine is well-suited for branding, editorial, digital interfaces, and any project that demands a modern, professional typographic presence.
